Frequently Asked Questions?

  • 1. Which industries can use AI and ML?
    AI and ML can be applied in e-commerce, healthcare, finance, logistics, real estate, and many other fields.
  • 2. How do you check the accuracy of models?
    Models are validated using testing datasets, performance metrics, and ongoing monitoring to maintain accuracy.
  • 3. Do I need large amounts of data?
    Not always. In some cases, smaller datasets are sufficient, and techniques like transfer learning or synthetic data can be used.
  • 4. What is the usual timeline for delivery?
    Starter: 2–4 weeks, Growth: 4–8 weeks, Business: 2–3 months, Enterprise: timeline based on project analysis.
  • 5. Will the solution integrate with my current software?
    Yes, AI and ML systems can be integrated with existing platforms such as CRM, ERP, or custom applications.
  • 6. What happens after delivery?
    Support and monitoring are included (1–12 months depending on the plan). Extended maintenance packages are also available.
