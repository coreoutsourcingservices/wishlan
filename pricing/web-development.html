Frequently Asked Questions?

  • 1. Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
    Yes — you can move between plans at any time. Content, design, and features will be migrated accordingly.
  • 2. How long does delivery take?
    • Basics: approx. 1-2 weeks
    • Growth: approx. 3-4 weeks
    • Business: 5-7 weeks
    • Enterprise: depends on complexity; we’ll give you a timeline after requirement gathering.
  • 3. How do you price custom features?
    Custom features (e.g., complex integrations, membership portals, booking systems) are quoted separately based on scope and effort.
  • 4. Is domain & hosting included?
    We include the domain (for 1 year) and/or hosting discounts in Growth / higher plans. Basics plan may require separate domain & hosting charges.
  • 5. What support do I get after launch?
    Free maintenance is included according to plan (1-12 months). After that, we offer ongoing support & maintenance packages.
  • 6. Can you build websites with advanced features like membership or booking?
    Yes, advanced features such as memberships, booking systems, and API integrations can be included in Business & Enterprise plans or added separately.
