Automated Calling & Predictive Dialer Services

At WishLan, we provide Auto Dialer systems that manage outbound calling, reduce idle agent time, and help you reach contacts efficiently. Our solutions focus on reliability, ease of use, and integration with your existing setup.

Why Choose Auto Dialer?

Auto Dialer & Predictive / Progressive dialer services can:

  • Reduce manual dialing and waiting times
  • Improve call campaign throughput
  • Manage leads and contacts systematically
  • Schedule calls and monitor performance in real time
  • Provide reporting and insights for decision making
Our Auto Dialer Methodology

At WishLan, we follow a structured approach to deliver high-quality designs:

  1. Requirement Collection – Understand call volume, campaign goals, and business workflow
  2. Design & Setup – Configure and install appropriate dialer type (predictive, progressive, etc.)
  3. Data Preparation – Load contact lists, remove duplicates, organize lists for call schedules
  4. Testing & Adjustment – Tune settings, pacing, intervals, agent allocation
  5. Deployment & Monitoring – Launch campaigns; monitor in real time; adjust for performance
  6. Support & Maintenance – Ongoing troubleshooting, updates, and reporting

Frequently Asked Questions?

  • 1. What types of dialers are available?
    We support Predictive Dialer, Progressive Dialer, and Manual / Scheduled Auto Dialer options, depending on your campaign needs.
  • 2. How many calls can I make per month?
    That depends on your plan: Starter is up to ~5,000, Growth up to 20,000, Business up to 100,000, and Enterprise is custom or 100,000+.
  • 3. Can I track call performance in real time?
    Yes. All plans include dashboards for monitoring active calls, agent availability, answered vs unanswered calls, and other metrics.
  • 4. Does the auto dialer integrate with my existing CRM or lead database?
    Yes. Integration is part of Growth & higher plans; custom integration can be arranged for Enterprise clients.
  • 5. Is there support for call filtering (e.g. skipping voicemails, busy signals)?
    Yes. The system handles busy signals, no-response and voicemail removal or filtering, especially in Growth and above plans.
  • 6. What happens after setup? How is maintenance handled?
    After deployment, we provide support & updates based on the plan’s maintenance period (1-12 months). We also offer optional maintenance contracts for longer-term usage.
