Frequently Asked Questions?

  • 1. Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
    Yes, you can switch anytime. Deliverables and features will scale accordingly.
  • 2. How long does delivery take?
    • Starter: approx. 1–2 weeks
    • Growth: approx. 2-3 weeks
    • Business: 4-6 weeks
    • Enterprise: depends on complexity (custom timeline after analysis).
  • 3. What file formats do you provide?
    All designs are delivered in high-resolution (JPG, PNG, PDF) and editable formats (AI, PSD, EPS) as needed.
  • 4. Do you provide branding packages?
    Yes, complete brand identity design (logos, stationery, guidelines) is available in Business & Enterprise plans or as an add-on.
  • 5. What support do I get after project delivery?
    Free design updates & minor edits are included (1–12 months depending on plan). Extended support can be purchased.
  • 6. Do you offer advanced design options like 3D or animation?
    Yes, advanced options such as 3D mockups, animations, and brand videos are available as add-ons.
