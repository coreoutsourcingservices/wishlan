CRM Services – Manage Customers, Close Deals & Grow Faster

At WishLan, we see CRM as more than just software—it’s a growth enabler. Our CRM solutions help businesses of all sizes simplify customer management, streamline sales, and build stronger relationships to close deals faster.

Why Choose CRM?

A structured CRM strategy helps you simplify business processes and drive better outcomes. With WishLan CRM, you can:

  • Centralise customer data for smarter decision-making
  • Capture, score, and track leads effectively
  • Streamline sales pipeline and improve closure rates
  • Automate follow-ups and reduce manual effort
  • Enhance customer service with ticketing and support features
  • Generate insights with advanced reporting and analytics
  • Scale your CRM as your business grows
Our CRM Methodology

We follow a clear and systematic process to deliver consistent, measurable results:

  1. Business Audit – Understanding your sales processes, team structure, and customer journey.
  2. Strategy & Planning – Customising CRM features to align with your objectives.
  3. Setup & Integration – Implementing CRM with APIs, telephony, and business tools.
  4. Automation & Optimisation – Setting up workflows, campaigns, and sales tracking.
  5. Analytics & Reporting – Providing actionable insights with custom dashboards.

Frequently Asked Questions?

  • 1. Are contracts required?
    No. All our CRM plans are flexible—you can start, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.
  • 2. Can I change my plan later?
    Yes, you can upgrade as your business grows. All data and workflows are migrated seamlessly.
  • 3. What integrations are supported?
    WishLan CRM integrates with Email, WhatsApp, Telephony, Payment Gateways, Zapier, and other third-party tools.
  • 4. Is onboarding included?
    Yes. Every plan includes onboarding, training, and free setup.
  • 5. Do you provide custom solutions??
    Absolutely. Our team can build tailored CRM features and workflows as per your business requirements.
  • 6. Is my data safe with WishLan CRM?
    Yes. We use secure cloud hosting, regular backups, and enterprise-grade encryption to ensure your business data is always safe and accessible.
