Secure Email-to-Fax Document Transmission

At WishLan, our Mail2Fax service lets you send and receive documents via fax through email, without needing physical fax machines. We focus on security, reliability, and ease of use.

Why Choose Mail2Fax?

Mail2Fax Services help your business by:

  • Eliminating costs of maintaining fax machines (equipment, paper, toner)
  • Allowing faxing from anywhere, through email
  • Maintaining document confidentiality through encryption
  • Ensuring delivery confirmation & status tracking
  • Reducing operational overhead for document transmission
Our Mail2Fax Methodology
  1. Requirement Analysis – Understand how many faxes, frequency, file types, security needs
  2. Setup & Integration – Configure email-to-fax platform and associate your email domain or account
  3. User Training & Onboarding – Guide users on how to compose/attach documents, specify recipient fax numbers, etc.
  4. Transmission & Monitoring – Send, monitor delivery status, handle failed transmissions or errors
  5. Security & Compliance – Ensure encryption, secure storage (if applicable), compliance with regulations for sensitive data
  6. Support & Maintenance – Ongoing customer support, updates, performance monitoring

Frequently Asked Questions?

  • 1. How does Mail2Fax work with WishLan?
    You compose an email, attach the document, enter the recipient’s fax number (with domain / format), send the email. We convert and deliver it as a fax.
  • 2. Is my document secure during transmission?
    Yes. Documents are encrypted during transfer, and access is controlled to protect sensitive information.
  • 3. Do I still need a physical fax machine?
    No. All sending and receiving can be done via email or our online portal.
  • 4. What file types are permitted for faxing?
    Common document types such as PDF, TIFF, DOC, DOCX are supported.
  • 5. Can I send many documents at once or schedule transmissions?
    Yes. Growth, Business, and Enterprise plans include bulk or batch faxing; Enterprise also supports scheduling or recurring faxing.
  • 6. What kind of support do I receive?
    Support is included per your plan (1-12 months). We provide setup help, troubleshooting, status monitoring, and customer service.
