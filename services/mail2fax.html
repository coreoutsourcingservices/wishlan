How Mail2Fax Services Work

The process is straightforward and user-friendly. Once you sign up with WishLan, you get access to a secure email-to-fax platform. Here’s how it works:

  1. Compose an Email – Create a new email and attach the document you want to fax.
  2. Provide the Fax Number - In lieu of an email address, provide the recipient's fax number and the provided domain.
  3. Send the Email – Your email is converted into a fax and delivered to the recipient’s fax machine.
  4. Receive Confirmation – You’ll get a delivery status notification for your records.

This streamlined process ensures your documents are delivered safely and efficiently.
