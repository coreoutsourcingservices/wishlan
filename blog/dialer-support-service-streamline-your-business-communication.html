What is a Predictive Dialer?

WishLanA predictive dialer uses advanced algorithms to dial multiple numbers at once. It predicts when an agent will be free and routes answered calls accordingly. This means less idle time and more connected conversations.

Advantages of Predictive Dialers:

  • Reduces agent waiting time
  • Connects only live calls (avoiding unanswered calls or busy tones)
  • Improves agent productivity
  • Scales efficiently for larger teams

Companies handling hundreds of outbound calls daily often choose predictive dialers to maximize performance.
