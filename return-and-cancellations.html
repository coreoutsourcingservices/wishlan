Return & Cancellations

Last updated: April 24, 2025

Please read these Return & Cancellations carefully before using Our Service.

Wishlan.com is an online marketplace that connects buyers and sellers. Our platform serves as a facilitator, allowing users to list, discover, and purchase a wide variety of products and services. We do not directly sell or own any of the products listed on the platform.

All sales, purchases, deliveries, and other transactions are conducted directly between users (i.e., buyers and sellers). Wishlan is not a party to any contract for the sale or supply of goods or services, and we do not guarantee the quality, legality, safety, or availability of the listed products or services

Returns & Cancellations

Since Wishlan is a neutral platform that connects buyers and sellers, we do not offer refunds, returns, or cancellations on behalf of any sellers.

Each seller may have their own individual return, refund, and cancellation policies. We recommend that users carefully review the seller's terms before making a purchase and communicate directly with the seller for any post-purchase concerns.

Wishlan does not:

Mediate or guarantee returns or cancellations;

Process refunds on behalf of sellers;

Take responsibility for incomplete or unsatisfactory transactions.

Dispute Resolution Between Users

We understand that disputes may occasionally arise. Wishlan encourages buyers and sellers to communicate directly and resolve any issues amicably.

If you need assistance or have concerns about your experience on Wishlan, you may contact our support team for guidance. While we cannot enforce resolutions, we’re here to help facilitate communication between users when needed.
